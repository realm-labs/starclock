- Black Forest Cuckoo Clock (`universe.curio.66`);

## Offer and domain-entry composition

Divination Cuckoo Clock reduces each post-battle Blessing offer by one visible
choice. The random-offer policy now accepts an ordered collection of checked
reductions rather than one optional reduction. It therefore composes with
Dimension Reduction Dice: an ordinary offer shows three choices, either Curio
reduces it to two, and both reduce it to one. Catalog validation rejects a
combination that could reduce an offer to zero.

Perpetual Motion Cuckoo Clock settles on every transition into a following
Domain. One atomic Activity branch subtracts
`floor(current Cosmic Fragments * 5 / 100)`, records `DomainEntered`, then
continues route traversal. Domain-entry Curios execute in stable Curio order:
Cogwheel (`10`), Gold Coin (`21`), then Perpetual Motion (`67`). No 3D scene,
room collision or encounter-specific branch is involved.

## Service costs

IPC Cuckoo Clock multiplies the Cosmic Fragment cost of Blessing enhancement
and Blessing-offer reset by 125%. Service payload revision 5 carries explicit
inflated and adjusted debit operations. When Faith Bond is also owned, IPC
inflation is applied first and the independent discount is applied second;
all integer rounding remains at the existing service boundary. Curio and
revival purchases are unaffected.

## Battle-entry rules

Black Forest Cuckoo Clock uses the authoritative behavior RNG stream to select
one stable living, present ally at battle start. A non-dispellable neutral
effect adds 500% of base Aggro for five target-turn ends. Released descriptions
publish only “greatly increases” and no numeric coefficient. The `+500%`
project policy (x6 total Aggro) is therefore a recorded, replaceable numeric
approximation; target selection, duration and lifecycle semantics are exact.

Mechanical Cuckoo Clock spends `min(2, current Skill Points)` at battle start.
The typed resource operation uses fixed-point scalar operands and floor
rounding, so a low balance never underflows.

Both mechanics attach through immutable Curio contributions and ordinary Rule
IR selectors, triggers, effects, modifiers and resource operations. They add
no Curio branch to combat-core and require no native handler.
