# Divergent Universe Tawot service

## Current boundary

`TawotServices` drives an explicitly admitted, executable purchase service.
It does not claim that its Curio candidates have complete effects. It defines four
Forge-level configurations for the current `Urban Maintenance (Tawot Cards)`
occurrence. Purchase menus, persisted offers, cancellation, paid selection and
repeat limits execute through shared Activity graph nodes. Automatic original
Forge-domain admission remains unimplemented.

The ordinary reward pool's canonical-first-state policy remains unchanged.
Randomizing that pool would not implement this service's paid three-way choice.
The public entry builder's `with_initial_tawot_service(level)` explicitly admits
the service after the initial checkpoint and before its battle. The optional
`flow_with_tawot_service` fixture uses this same API: the public Color reward
can fund the service. The default baseline remains unchanged. This provides a
policy-bound producer for all twelve states, including 9069, without claiming
original room placement or automatic Forge generation.

## Executed policy

`VersionedProjectPolicyUniformDistinctCurrentStatesPaidSelection` declares:

| Forge level | Price per selection | Distinct candidates shown | Purchase limit per service visit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2 | 100 | 3 | 1 |
| 3 | 100 | 3 | 1 |
| 4 | 100 | 3 | 2 |
| 5 | 100 | 3 | 2 |

- The owning Forge graph must admit the service at a declared level. An
  unbound trusted acquisition call is not that admission.
- Sample all current states of the bound handbook uniformly, without
  replacement, from stable-key order using a labeled Activity RNG stream.
  Do not filter away states merely because their effects remain incomplete.
- Charge and increment the purchase count atomically with the chosen state's
  replacement/acquisition, not opening the offer. Preflight mandatory rewards.
- Free cancellation preserves the sampled offer; reopening cannot reroll it.
  Replacement may select the existing state and initializes a fresh allowance.
- Persist offer and count in authoritative service-local Activity state.
  Leaving discards them; a separately admitted visit starts a new allowance.
- The level-5 Towat boss-card service remains a separate incomplete mechanism.

Every hidden choice has low parity confidence and an independent replacement
condition in the workbook. Alternatives include payment before reveal,
excluding owned states and cancellation forfeiture. Current policy must be
replaced field-by-field when released graphs or reproducible observations
establish stronger evidence.

## Execution integration and remaining boundaries

The factory also exposes `tawot_room_compiler(service_level)` and
`compile_tawot_room(context, service_level)` for explicit placement in the current
[position graph](divergent-universe-domain-layout.md). The reusable compiler
shares only immutable service definitions; each graph fragment has its own
source-position namespace and logical room instance. Its accepted menu/card
commands use the same payment, sampling, replacement and acquisition executor
as the existing initial-checkpoint service, not a second service state machine.
There is no implicit battle after leaving this fragment: it continues to the
next exact source position supplied by the route compiler.

This API does **not** infer original Forge admission. All nine current authored
decks contain level-one Reforge cards, whereas the existing Tawot policy only
covers service levels 2–5. The required caller-selected service level remains
independent of the preset's level; no card is upgraded, relabeled or silently
given level-two eligibility. Automatic level-one Forge content and the public
baseline's production position binding remain incomplete. The factory rejects
service levels without authored definitions.

The owning profile replaces its four service declarations with the fragment's
exact declarations: purchases (62), cached offer (63) and opening budget (65)
bind to the logical room, while the acceptance gate (64) resets on physical node
entry. Internal menu/card loops preserve counters and cache; another room in the
same plane starts a separate allowance. Initialization and accepted exit both
clear service-local state. The fragment reserves 64 openings and a final Leave
path through bounded shared graph nodes and edges.

`CompiledTawotRoom::bind` validates exact programs, nodes, internal edges,
successful continuation, logical paths, slot declarations and absence of added
room-offer policies. `BoundTawotRoom` accepts only offered IDs from that immutable
whole graph; it also checks separately reconstructed definitions structurally.
The whole-profile `position_room_identity`/`bind_position_rooms` boundary now
combines these services with exact battle fragments. Each selected service level
and placement binds current configuration identity, including levels with the
same menu shape. The existing flow service APIs and headless controller dispatch
to the authenticated room capability. Source-context joins are validated before
compilation; this does not infer original NPC eligibility. External edges into
menu/cards and added room random checkpoints reject along with offer policies.
Accepted commands return their shared transaction events. Hidden/raw/stale/foreign
selections and downstream exit-initialization failures preserve authoritative
state, cached offers, scopes, events and RNG.

Position-graph tests execute actual paid purchases, cancellation, candidate
ownership and independent repeated visits for both run families and all four
authored service policies. Other rooms in these tests are explicit route probes;
starting fragments are controlled resource fixtures. Their successful test
terminals are not original room gameplay, encoded profile replay, automatic NPC
membership or complete-run release evidence. No additional source obligation is
terminalized by this composition.

Combined-profile tests additionally pay for repeated source-position visits with
actual proxy battle income, without a controlled starting-currency grant. Both
families and all four levels reconstruct the same choices, ownership, carry and
terminal from fresh definitions. This removes the former service/battle slot
composition gap, not the original Forge admission or encoded-position replay gap.

Shared graph transitions are `checkpoint → menu ⇄ cards`, followed by
`menu → encounter → real battle`. The checkpoint retains its occurrence
grant/finish/door ordering. The extra encounter node resolves the existing first
encounter policy and immutable assembly; it is not a second battle engine.

Service exit clears the cache and counters. The initial-entry binding
admits one visit in the first section. Independently placed repeat visits are
available through the explicit position-profile construction boundary, not
through the default CLI/Agent/MCP entry or encoded-position replay.
Menu/card loops preserve the logical first-domain
address and never consume Curio domain lifetimes. A technical bound permits
64 openings per visit, then disables Buy while retaining Leave. Menu/card visit,
edge and total graph budgets reserve the return and exit path. This is a
project execution bound, not observed gameplay parity.

Visible option authentication and current-funds checks precede generation.
Payment, reacquisition, source-backed mandatory grants, allowance updates,
option consumption and automatic graph pumping share one transaction. A stale
menu remains a snapshot after rejected commands; re-entering the menu rebuilds
eligibility. Raw graph choices cannot bypass generated acquisition acceptance.
Same-state purchases reactivate destroyed holdings and initialize fresh charges.

The headless controller routes these choices through the same service boundary.
CLI `universe run --mode divergent-universe --seed 24121
--tawot-forge-level 2` explicitly requests the service. Agent session creation
and MCP `starclock_create_universe` accept optional `tawot_forge_level` as a
canonical unsigned decimal string from `"2"` through `"5"`. Omission preserves
the default entry. MCP rejects this field for other modes; invalid levels are
rejected before session creation. CLI run and verification output identify the
selected level (JSON `null` when absent). These are policy-bound entry inputs,
not claims of automatic Forge topology.

The current encoded replay begins with an accepted entry command and its
expected initial Activity state hash. Its `DUE` payload contains the exact
area and difficulty (bounded UTF-8 strings with little-endian u32 byte lengths)
and an optional service level (little-endian u16, zero means absent).
Verification rebuilds those inputs from current production definitions and
checks entry/component digests before executing any recorded player selection.
It does not infer configuration from an opaque hash, substitute default choices,
or support an old-format decoder. Player action counts exclude entry creation.
Malformed, missing or changed inputs and an altered initial hash are rejected.

The replay action guard adds the admitted service's bounded menu/card budget
to the default controller budget. This permits every legal 64-open cancellation
sequence and its exit without turning the 32-step default controller limit into
a restriction on player-authored transcripts. The shared graph still enforces
visit and edge budgets. CLI fresh-process and Agent/MCP exported replays use the
same reconstruction path. Automatic Forge topology, independently generated
repeat visits and the separate level-5 Towat service remain pending.

Data tests currently cover exact joins, four level definitions, all twelve
candidates and malformed identity, level, cost, width, limit, provenance and
policy fields. Runtime tests cover all twelve states' actual paid acquisition,
cancel/reopen determinism, insufficient current funds, stale/unoffered commands,
raw-choice rejection, same-state replacement, limits and the safe-exit budget.
Both run families and all four levels complete their three real proxy battles
and compare each later boundary with fresh reconstruction. A publicly purchased
9069 is also destroyed with a controlled remaining allowance, repurchased and
verified to consume its refreshed counter after a real battle.
A public purchase of 9073 binds its [final-damage passive](divergent-universe-curio-battle-stats.md),
with actual damage probes, three-battle completion and fresh encoded replay in
both run families. This does not establish five-battle public exhaustion.
Publicly purchased 9071 also grants its exact 60 fragments at each of the two
future public domain choices, with fresh encoded replay in both run families.
Its third-entry grant/discard is covered by separate controlled vectors, not
claimed as a three-entry public lifetime.
Publicly purchased 9072 grants an [immediate victory Blessing](divergent-universe-tawot-victory.md)
after each actual battle while active with positive domain allowance. Its public
three-battle replay and separate domain-expiry vectors do not establish original
Forge topology or a full three-entry public lifetime.
No source obligation or mechanic program receives terminal credit: complete
candidate effects and original owning-domain construction remain separate gaps.
