## Executable policy

`VersionedProjectPolicyOtherShieldAppliedOwnerTurnExcessDecay` binds immutable
mapped original-party Paths. Any Preservation member enables all original party
recipients; later provider death does not recalculate eligibility. Ownership
selectors exclude linked actors even if bundles are inherited. Living present
recipients receive one permanent, non-dispellable, battle-scoped marker at
`BattleStarted` / `AfterEvent`, initially without positive shield capacity.

Each positive ordinary `Shield Applied` event transfers its actual post-formula
capacity once at `AfterMutation`. Repeated or replacement grants are independent
events. Missing markers are installed before transfer. Exact shared
`AdjustEffectShield::Increase` does not reapply shield bonuses, replace the shield
identity, refresh the marker or recurse through another ordinary grant. Special
and ordinary shields coexist under `ConcurrentLargest` absorption.

Once per recipient `TurnStarted` / `AfterEvent`, the program reads that owner's
special capacity and live maximum HP from one immutable snapshot. The loss is
`floor(0.9 * max(special - 0.3 * maximum_hp, 0))`; threshold precision is retained
until the final capacity boundary. For maximum HP 101 and special capacity 41,
the threshold is 30.3, the loss is 9 and the result is 32, not 31.

A separate `AfterMutation` reaction accepts only negative `Adjusted` deltas for
this special definition, then heals `floor(0.1 * actual_loss)` without healing
modifiers. It cannot heal from absorption, ordinary shields, capacity increases
or removal. Explicit marker removal clears only its capacity when no replacement
marker exists. No Activity mutation, RNG or separate combat state machine is used.
Equipment changes affect future battle snapshots, not previously assembled battles.

Roster/birth scope, exact event order, replacement grants, absorption policy,
live-HP threshold, rounding, modifier treatment and teardown are low-confidence
policy, **not observed parity**. Alternatives include additive absorption,
per-caster layers, initial HP-based capacity, summon inheritance, prematurely
floored thresholds or boosted healing. Replace fields independently with released
programs or reproducible current observations; preserve all four exact operands.
