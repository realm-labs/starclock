# Divergent Universe Tawot victory Blessing

State 9072 has an exact one-random-Blessing victory component and a three-domain
discard limit. `CurioVictoryBlessings` defines the grant independently from its
`CurioDomainExpiries` lifetime. It shares the Sage executor without changing
the robe's domain restrictions or imposing a domain counter on those forms.

The source establishes the count, victory trigger and domain limit, not an exact
rarity distribution or hidden interactions.
`VersionedProjectPolicyPositiveDomainAllowanceUniformUnownedAvailableSubset`
selects the active current holding with positive remaining domain allowance.
Every current mode-owned battle-domain label (Combat, Elite, Aberration, Boss)
is admitted; that does not prove original domain placement or enemy semantics.

The policy selects the current unowned base 1–3-star Blessing pool with unit
weights, stable state/identity ordering and no replacement across all simultaneous
Curio victory grants. Enhanced holdings exclude their base identity. It reuses
Reward purpose 24051 and ordinary post-battle active suppression states; sealing
wax offer weights do not affect this immediate pool. Empty pools grant and draw
nothing. The rarity range, pool, weighting, suppression and exhaustion are
independent low-confidence policies, not exact observed parity. Alternatives
include weighted/fixed rarity pools, duplicate conversion, unsuppressed intrinsic
rewards and once-per-domain timing; replace each field independently when released
execution or reproducible observations establish it.

## Lifetime and transaction

The existing [future-domain expiry policy](divergent-universe-curio-domain-expiry.md)
initializes three entries without counting the already entered acquisition domain.
The third future entry discards before encounter construction and that domain's
battle rewards. Battles and internal graph nodes do not consume domain allowance.
Destruction pauses both benefit and count; repair resumes; replacement/reacquisition
refill. A trusted zero allowance cannot grant and is removed at the next entry.

Verified wins grant in stage 24050 before battle-lifetime settlement and normal
Blessing candidate generation. Acquisition executes no victory reward. Loss,
fault, rejected and duplicate results do not grant. Inventory and Equation refresh
commit with carry, all later settlement stages, graph advancement and RNG in one
transaction. Normal offers exclude newly granted identities. A later rejection
restores the entire pre-result state, including pending result and random streams.

## Verification boundary

Tests exercise actual inventory, all policy rarities, empty pools, coexistence
with Sage, post-grant offer exclusion, destroy/repair/replacement, zero allowance,
nonvictory projections, duplicate rejection and late rollback. The four domain
labels have controlled policy vectors; they are not four original domain graphs.
Separate three-entry vectors verify discard and loss of the next battle's reward.

Both run families publicly buy 9072, receive one immediate Blessing after each of
three actual proxy battles, choose two future domains and verify encoded replay
against a fresh production fixture. This is not a full three-future-entry public
lifetime, original Forge placement or completed source-program disposition.
